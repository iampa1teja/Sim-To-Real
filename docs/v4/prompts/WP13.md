# WP13 — stack_bowls and the procedural bowl assets (Codex prompt)

## Goal

Add the task `stack_bowls`: put the small bowl into the large bowl, with scripted successes, failures and recoveries, like the other target tasks. This package carries the highest physics risk (concave shapes under Newton), so it comes last and its geometry is a proposal for the owner to confirm.

## Acceptance


pass and every earlier test still passes. Container: expert success ≥ 80 % on the SO-101 and stable nesting under Newton. Do not edit, skip or weaken tests; if one contradicts the contract, stop and report.

## Decisions (pinned by the tests)

- Objects `small_bowl` (target) and `large_bowl` (container), both USD objects with `origin = "bottom"`; small bowl `size_outer = [0.04, 0.04, 0.025]` (the gripper closes on its body: width ≤ `[arm.gripper].max_object_width_m`), large bowl `size_outer = [0.08, 0.08, 0.035]`, `inner_half_size = [0.03, 0.03]`, `floor_z = 0.003`. Instruction "Put the small bowl into the large bowl".
- Success: `nested = all(nested_in(@target in @container), released)`, `success = all(nested, ee_clear)`.
- USDA: each file starts with `#usda 1.0`, declares `defaultPrim`, and its default prim has `float3[] extent = [(-r, -r, 0), (r, r, h)]` matching `size_outer`. Geometry: a revolved bowl (flat floor, sloped wall, rim) as a mesh for rendering, and for collision a floor disc plus N ≥ 12 convex wall segments around the rim (Newton needs convex pieces). Rigid body with the mass from `task.toml`.
- Same pitfalls as the stack task: keep `over_bowl` out of `hold_until`, and keep the `raised` threshold at 0.01 m.
